Home is how your real board lies on the table. Reset always comes back to it, and the sliders tilt it from there as you see it on screen. A real chip follows the right-hand rule, so with Z pointing down, X into the screen means Y points right, and Y left means X points toward you. Pick the one your edge test shows.

Chip range

A bigger range fits bigger values but gives fewer counts per g. Watch the raw numbers change. Too small a range and the value gets stuck at the edge: that is called clipping.

Show

Virtual people sit at a table with the pendant on the chest. Each bend is a lean toward the plate at a random angle inside your bracket, toward a random spot on the plate, at that person's own speed, before they sit back up. They also sit still, talk, look at a phone, walk, jog, run, bend to the floor and turn in the chair, so the model learns what is not a bend. Every sample is labelled, and the files are laid out the way MEMS Studio expects.
